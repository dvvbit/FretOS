FretOS - play Clone Hero on a Mac with a wired Xbox 360 guitar

HOW TO SET IT UP (about 2 minutes, only once)

1. Drag FretOS into your Applications folder.

2. Double-click FretOS to open it.
   macOS will say it "could not verify" FretOS. That's normal for free apps
   that aren't registered with Apple. To open it anyway:

   macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer:
     - Click "Done".
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     - Scroll down. Next to "FretOS was blocked", click "Open Anyway".
     - Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" again.

   macOS 13 or 14:
     - Right-click (or Control-click) FretOS and choose "Open".
     - Click "Open".

3. Plug your guitar into a USB port.

4. FretOS asks to press keys for you. Click the yellow button
   (it says OPEN SETTINGS or ALLOW).
   System Settings opens to Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
   Turn on the switch next to FretOS. Enter your password if asked.
   Go back to FretOS and click REOPEN.

5. Press the green fret. If the green box lights up, you're done.
   Open Clone Hero and play.

FretOS uses Clone Hero's default keyboard keys, so you don't need to
change anything in Clone Hero.

Help and updates: see the GitHub page you downloaded this from.
